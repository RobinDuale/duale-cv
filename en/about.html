About

Who better than an experienced leader
to support another leader?

Discover my track record

After 18 years growing companies as Managing Director and CEO of nine businesses, I know the responsibility of the person who decides, often alone, and how hard it is to find a truly independent counterpart, able to challenge their convictions, with no interest other than the success of the leader and the company.

What I have lived through

I have lived through the decisions that shape a company's future: finding new growth drivers, transforming a business model, completing and integrating acquisitions, managing crises, leading reorganisations, making trade-offs and reporting to shareholders, whether family or financial.

Turning around Éditions Tissot, with revenue grown from €6M to €10M and EBITDA margin from 7% to 23%. Moving Techniques de l'Ingénieur to full subscription, with NRR tripled. Building with Lexagri a global leader in agricultural regulatory data, then selling it. Integrating 3E France into an international group, with 100% of customers migrated to subscription. In total, 5 acquisitions integrated and 6 divestments.

Sparring partner at VISCONTI Partners

I chose to put this experience at the service of other leaders. I am an executive coach and sparring partner at VISCONTI Partners, where I support the leaders of startups, SMEs and mid-market companies.

My promise: to unlock your potential so you can better serve your company and society. Together we build a peer-to-peer relationship over the long term. It is a space of trust, freedom of speech and high standards, to step back, test your options and move forward in a continuous cycle of reflection, decision and action.

My support pursues three objectives:

Leadership craft
Grow in your role as a leader, together with your leadership team.
Company value
Build lasting value for your company.
Fulfilment
Foster your individual fulfilment and that of your team.

Digital and artificial intelligence

My background in software, data and digital services also gives me expertise in digital transformation and artificial intelligence.

In professional publishing, I have already led a transformation of this scale: the shift from print to digital. Everything had to be reinvented: production, product, distribution, business model. Those who failed to transform disappeared. AI is now putting those same companies in the same existential situation. Having led through the first wave gives a rare understanding of the levers to activate for the second.
